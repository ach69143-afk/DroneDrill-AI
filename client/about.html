DRONEDRILL AI - SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & METHODOLOGY OFFLINE PROTOTYPE

DroneDrill AI is an offline, adaptive counter-UAS training architecture. It ingests procedural or simulated MAVLink-style telemetry, executes Signal Detection Theory and Bayesian skill tracking, and isolates memorization from genuine threat recognition via held-out generalization testing.

3. HELD-OUT GENERALIZATION & ROTE GAP EXPLAINED 80 / 20 FACTORIAL SPLIT

Standard military and industrial simulators frequently fall victim to memorization bias: operators practice identical terrain, weather, and sensor presets repeatedly until high scores reflect familiarity with the drill rather than genuine threat recognition.

To resolve this, DroneDrill AI divides the 540-cell condition factor space into:

  • Practice Split (80% / 432 cells): Available for procedural adaptive training and scripted library drills.
  • Held-Out Test Split (20% / 108 cells): Strictly quarantined from all practice, recommendation, and instructor builder drills.

In Test Mode, an operator is presented exclusively with held-out condition combinations. The system then computes the Rote Gap:

Rote Gap = Practice Mean Score - Held-Out Test Mean Score

A near-zero Rote Gap proves that the operator has learned robust visual and kinematic principles that transfer reliably to novel, unseen threat situations. A high gap reveals overfitting to training templates.

PROTOTYPE LIMITATIONS & OPERATIONAL BOUNDARIES
  • Abstract Scenarios: Visual models and kinematics are synthetic geometric approximations designed for cognitive recognition training; not a certified military simulation.
  • Decision Tree SOP: Response appropriateness weights and threat categories are instructor-editable drafts pending formal military doctrine and expert review.
  • Difficulty Weights: Condition factor difficulty indices are analytical estimates that require empirical calibration through human subject trials.
  • Pilot Study: No human operator pilot study has been conducted yet; performance curves represent preliminary synthetic agent benchmarks.
  • Synthetic Trainees: Roster data and cohort historical profiles reflect synthetic demonstration agents.
